<objective>
Drop the GRID line from the workspace QuickPick in `pickAndExchangeWorkspace`. The current picker shows GRID as the `description` line and `authId: <raw>` as the `detail` line; both expose internal plumbing. The new picker shows workspace name as `label` and raw `authId` (no prefix) as `description`. No third line.

Output: `src/workspace.ts` with simplified QuickPick item construction in `pickAndExchangeWorkspace`.
</objective>
