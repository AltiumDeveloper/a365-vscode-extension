Ship the three observable user-facing wins that have no dependency on the title-bar submenu or execution-routing refactors:

1. Single-click on a script in the side panel opens it for Edit (D-19, SC-4).

Sequencing this plan first avoids merge churn (RESEARCH §5.6).
