## Wave Structure Rationale

Initial intent (per RESEARCH §Phase Plan Outline) was a 5-wave structure with some parallelism. **All 6 plans are serial in execution order** because plans 02, 03, 04, and 06 all edit `src/extension.ts`. Per the planner's `assign_waves` rule (same-wave plans must have zero `files_modified` overlap), this collapses to strict serial. The parallelism trade-off was not worth a refactor of activate() into discrete subscription-list builders — flagged as a backlog candidate (see below).

## Deviations from RESEARCH Recommendations

1. **5 waves → 6 waves (serial).** RESEARCH proposed parallelism between FSP (wave 3) and command UX (wave 4). File ownership of `src/extension.ts` forces them serial. Backlog candidate: refactor activate() to enable future parallelism (see below).
2. **D-17 wording.** RESEARCH suggested amending CONTEXT.md to clarify the "save-bridge" terminology. We did NOT amend CONTEXT.md (it's a locked decision artifact); instead, Plan 03 puts an inline Pitfall-6 comment in `src/testEvents/eventFs.ts` that records the clarification at the code level. CONTEXT.md unchanged.
3. **Plan 04 delete-default policy** is left to the implementer (clear `defaultName` to undefined vs auto-promote first remaining event). Plan 04 SUMMARY records the choice — both are acceptable per D-07 wording.
